The problem

Your audience is the hardest audience there is

Everything you publish is read by engineers who will spot a wrong claim in the first paragraph and quietly stop trusting the rest of it — including the product page.

That's why hardware content is so hard to outsource. A generalist writer produces something fluent and subtly wrong. A domain expert who doesn't write produces something correct and unreadable. Both cost you credibility with exactly the people you were trying to reach.

I do both halves. Every technical claim I publish under your name, I've verified against the spec, the RTL, the datasheet, or the tool output — and I can defend it in your comments section.
